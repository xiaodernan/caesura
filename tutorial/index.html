Thirty seconds · three lights

Tap three cards → light three pips → you already know how to fight
三盏灯全亮。你现在已经能打第一场遭遇了——按 Esc 暂停、按 F 打开手册，遇到不懂的看图鉴。完整教程在下面的 14 步；但你先打前三步，就不会卡。 Three pips lit. You can already fight the first encounter — Esc pauses, F opens the in-game manual, the codex answers everything else. The 14-step primer sits below; but your first three steps already carry you.
01
开弦
第一次进入游戏，标题会出若干出身卡（决定初武器/被动）。按下 ← → 挑卡，Enter 确定——出身会钉死，再也换不了。这不是数值选择，是一段人生。
挑你不熟的那张——你已经知道自己怎么打了
Struck
On your first run the title deals out origin cards (starting weapon / passive). Pick one with ← →, confirm with Enter — the pick is final. It is not a stat choice; it is a life.
Pick the card you don't know — you already know how you fight
02
横拖
WASD 移动。左键 LMB 轻击，右键 RMB 重击。Shift 冲刺，Space 跳跃，Ctrl 偏闪——三分攻，七分缓。
慢是常态，急是风险
Drift-string
WASD to move. LMB light, RMB heavy. Shift to sprint, Space to leap, Ctrl to dodge — three parts strike, seven parts pace.
Slow is the default; haste is the risk
03
盾锁与反击
F 格挡，G 弹反。弹反成功会让对面踉跄进入脆弱窗口——Boss 也一样。先弹反，后挥砍。
后发先至 · 无轻不重
Guard & Parry
F to guard, G to parry. A landed parry staggers the foe into a real vulnerability window — bosses too. Parry first, then strike.
Strike after the drum, heavy after light
04
锁定
靠近对手按 Q 锁定，多按几次切锁；Q 再按一次放掉。锁定后视角跟着它走，打得稳。
锁定半屏，弱点一眼看穿
Lock-on
Approach a foe, press Q to lock on, press again to switch targets, press once more to release. Your view follows it — your hands stay honest.
Lock for half a screen, see its weak point at a glance
05
音色相生
武器的每一击都在唱。按 V 停下来听一听——三秒世界一静，所有人的调性暴露。
V · 听世界 · 三秒后再动
Kith of Tone
Every swing of your weapon sings. Press V to stop and listen — for three seconds the world goes quiet and every voice shows its pitch.
V · listen · three seconds, then move
06
耳鸣
听到脚下石屑、远处铃震、魔物呻吟？那是它听到你了。声音=你在哪，声音=敌在哪。沉默是唯一安全的语言。
多一步回响，多一记耳光
Tinnitus
Hear gravel? A far bell? A groan from the dark? That is the world knowing you. Sound is where you are; sound is where it is. In halls of echo, silence is the only safe tongue.
One step too many and the bells remember
07
寄存
随时按 Esc 暂停。菜单里三件事：继续 / 调出锻造 / 存档+退出。新手最好的朋友是 Esc。
不需要记敌人位置——只需记得 Esc 在哪
Shelve
Press Esc any time to pause. The menu offers three things: resume · the tuning peg · save and quit. Esc is a new player's best friend.
You don't need to remember where it is — just remember Esc
08
Y 轴
鼠标上滑＝抬眼向上看（FPS 标准）。不合你手？按 Esc →「操作」页 → 「鼠标 Y 轴反转」行——翻转就是 Unity 口径。此格是会话级偏好，不入档，每次启动都回落到 FPS 标准。滚轮：往上滑＝视线往上走、往下滑＝俯视往下看——不动手也能抬头看天、低头看海。
不习惯就翻一下——随时能翻回来
Y Axis
Mouse up = look up (FPS norm). Not your hand? Press Esc → Operations page → "Mouse Y-axis invert" — flips to the Unity convention. Session-scoped: never saved, every launch falls back to FPS norm. Scroll wheel: scroll up = eyes up, scroll down = gaze down — look up at the sky without moving the mouse.
Flip it any time — it flips back just as easily
09
手册 · 游记
还需要更多？游戏里按 Tab，调音桩翻到末尾的 「手册 / Manual」——开局导航、三场 Boss 提要、结局走向；再翻一格是 「游记 / Atlas」——八区指北针、boss 在哪、刻痕在哪。它们就是这款游戏的「说明书 + 世界地图」，都住在调音桩里。
它不挑场合——什么时候想看都翻得到
Manual · Atlas
Want more? Press Tab in-game; flip to the "Manual" tab at the end of the peg — the opening route, field notes on each boss, ending paths; one more flip lands on "Atlas" — all eight reaches, boss locations, and where every carved mark lives. Those two are the game's own manual and world map — both live in the tuning peg.
Open either any time — no conditions
10
状态图与灵魂
每个 NPC、每条主线 flag、每条感情线——都是一张状态图。一个 NPC 的对话树就是 StateGraph(Nodes, Edges)，条件边是 advanceOn.flags：你敲一下、写一行 flag、她就会走向下一格。 你读到的每一句台词，背后都是一条从上一个状态指向下一个状态的边。LangGraph 把这种东西画成 DAG——本作的 dialogue.json 就是一张等价的有向图，只是不喊出名字而已。
重写一张状态图 = 换一条走线；推进条件就是一枚能拧回去的 flag
想读懂这张图？翻开 世界谱 — 搜索「状态图 StateGraph」即可看到每一条边的定义
State graphs and souls
Every NPC, every story flag, every thread in the game — is a state graph. A conversation tree is StateGraph(Nodes, Edges); advanceOn.flags are the conditional edges: strike once, set a flag, and she steps one cell forward. Every line you read is a directed edge from one state to the next. LangGraph draws these as DAGs — this game's dialogue.json is the same object without the branding.
Rewriting one graph = redrawing its line; changing one advance flag = rerouting a soul
11
世界谱 · 搜什么
失忆？问「万籁」。迷路？问「休垒」。不信这世界有道理？在 世界谱 搜索状态图 StateGraph——每一条边都告诉你：她为什么这么走、这个结局为什么这样收尾。全语言：中文搜一次、英文再搜一次，答案一样。
搜一字也能搜——「灯」「钟」「门」「结」都行
The Codex · what to search
Forgot everything? Ask The Myriad. Lost? Ask Resthold. Think this world makes no sense? Search World Codex for StateGraph — every edge tells you why she walked that way and why the ending ended that way. Search in Chinese once, English once: same answer either way.
One character works too — 灯 / 钟 / 门 / 结
12
三语：简体 / 繁體 / EN
右上角那颗按钮轮流切换 简体 / 繁體 / English。止音纪元的中文只有一种声音——同一把敲钟的口气，只是写法随你读哪一端：大陆人看简体，岛内人看正体，外语人看英语。
繁體模式下切换再切回来——中文变体来回不会跳字
Three languages · one voice
The button at the top right cycles Simplified / Traditional / English. The game speaks in one Chinese register — same chipped-bell cadence, just written for whichever ear reads it: Simplified for mainland shores, Traditional for the island, English for everybody else.
Switch Traditional → back → back — no glyph skipping
13
这世界怎么成的：AI 导演
你手上这份剧本不是一个人写的——它是一整条导演流水线咬出来的：世界检索（RAG，现算 1300+ 个 canon 片段）→ 一致性审计（最近一次真机跑：52/100——模型自己挑了三条软肋）→ 状态流转提案 → 篇章草稿。全程留档，没有一步替你拍板：模型只在打草稿时在场；你游玩时零 LLM、零网络。后台「止音后台（口令 123）」那页摆着最近一次真机运行的原始产物——包括模型给自己判的缺口和淘汰案。
想看这条流水线的轨迹？进后台翻到「状态图检查站」——七个节点，每一步都是真跑（图里只有运行轨迹，没有任何密钥）
How this world got made · the AI Director
The script you are holding was not written by one hand — it was chewed out of a whole production line: canon retrieval (RAG over 1300+ fragments) → consistency audit (the last on-machine run scored 52/100 — the model flagged its own three softest spots) → state-transition proposals → a chapter draft. All archived; no step decided for you. The model only attends at the drafting table. While you play it is zero LLM, zero network. The Observatory (passphrase 123) shows the raw artifacts of its latest run — including the gaps the model found itself.
Want the trajectory? Open the Observatory → “StateGraph checkpoints” — seven nodes, every step a real run
一句给玩家的备忘
止音纪元不送你一把斩钉截铁的胜利——它送你一口铃、一个耳鸣、和一笔账。好好吹一吹那一口铃，玩家不用去看什么百科。
A reminder from the author
CAESURA does not hand you a clean victory — it hands you a bell, a ringing in your ears, and a ledger. Go play both hands of that bell; you should not have to read any encyclopedia to finish it.
