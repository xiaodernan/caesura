CAESURA · Player's Primer

Ring every key once — all you need is these few minutes

Start a fresh run Systems console Full guide
01
开弦
第一次进入游戏，标题会出若干出身卡（决定初武器/被动）。按下 ← → 挑卡，Enter 确定——出身会钉死，再也换不了。这不是数值选择，是一段人生。
挑你不熟的那张——你已经知道自己怎么打了
Struck
On your first run the title deals out origin cards (starting weapon / passive). Pick one with ← →, confirm with Enter — the pick is final. It is not a stat choice; it is a life.
Pick the card you don't know — you already know how you fight
02
横拖
WASD 移动。左键 LMB 轻击，右键 RMB 重击。Shift 冲刺，Space 跳跃，Ctrl 偏闪——三分攻，七分缓。
慢是常态，急是风险
Drift-string
WASD to move. LMB light, RMB heavy. Shift to sprint, Space to leap, Ctrl to dodge — three parts strike, seven parts pace.
Slow is the default; haste is the risk
03
盾锁与反击
F 格挡，G 弹反。弹反成功会让对面踉跄进入脆弱窗口——Boss 也一样。先弹反，后挥砍。
后发先至 · 无轻不重
Guard & Parry
F to guard, G to parry. A landed parry staggers the foe into a real vulnerability window — bosses too. Parry first, then strike.
Strike after the drum, heavy after light
04
锁定
靠近对手按 Q 锁定，多按几次切锁；Q 再按一次放掉。锁定后视角跟着它走，打得稳。
锁定半屏，弱点一眼看穿
Lock-on
Approach a foe, press Q to lock on, press again to switch targets, press once more to release. Your view follows it — your hands stay honest.
Lock for half a screen, see its weak point at a glance
05
音色相生
武器的每一击都在唱。按 V 停下来听一听——三秒世界一静，所有人的调性暴露。
V · 听世界 · 三秒后再动
Kith of Tone
Every swing of your weapon sings. Press V to stop and listen — for three seconds the world goes quiet and every voice shows its pitch.
V · listen · three seconds, then move
06
耳鸣
听到脚下石屑、远处铃震、魔物呻吟？那是它听到你了。声音=你在哪，声音=敌在哪。沉默是唯一安全的语言。
多一步回响，多一记耳光
Tinnitus
Hear gravel? A far bell? A groan from the dark? That is the world knowing you. Sound is where you are; sound is where it is. In halls of echo, silence is the only safe tongue.
One step too many and the bells remember
07
寄存
随时按 Esc 暂停。菜单里三件事：继续 / 调出锻造 / 存档+退出。新手最好的朋友是 Esc。
不需要记敌人位置——只需记得 Esc 在哪
Shelve
Press Esc any time to pause. The menu offers three things: resume · the tuning peg · save and quit. Esc is a new player's best friend.
You don't need to remember where it is — just remember Esc
08
Y 轴
鼠标上滑＝抬眼向上看（FPS 标准）。不合你手？按 Esc →「操作」页 → 「鼠标 Y 轴反转」行——翻转就是 Unity 口径。此格是会话级偏好，不入档，每次启动都回落到 FPS 标准。
不习惯就翻一下——随时能翻回来
Y Axis
Mouse up = look up (FPS norm). Not your hand? Press Esc → Operations page → "Mouse Y-axis invert" — flips to the Unity convention. Session-scoped: never saved, every launch falls back to FPS norm.
Flip it any time — it flips back just as easily
09
手册
还需要更多？游戏里按 Tab，调音桩翻到末尾的 「手册 / Manual」——它一会儿会告诉你：开局导航、三场 Boss 提要、结局走向——和你此刻该干什么。这就是这款游戏的「说明书」，它在调音桩里，不在别的地方。
它不挑场合——什么时候想看都翻得到
Manual
Want more? Press Tab in-game; flip to the "Manual" tab at the end of the peg — it tells you: the opening route, field notes on each boss, ending paths. That's the game's own manual — it lives in the tuning peg, not in some other file.
Open it any time — no conditions
一句给玩家的备忘
止音纪元不送你一把斩钉截铁的胜利——它送你一口铃、一个耳鸣、和一笔账。好好吹一吹那一口铃，玩家不用去看什么百科。
A reminder from the author
CAESURA does not hand you a clean victory — it hands you a bell, a ringing in your ears, and a ledger. Go play both hands of that bell; you should not have to read any encyclopedia to finish it.
