页角自翻

The Corner Turns

回廊钟第37年 · 傍晚，薄簿合着，页角偶尔自己翻动 · 她没有记下 · 有些淡去，不必补记

The 37th year of the Arcade — at dusk, the ledger stayed shut; the page corner turned now and then; she recorded nothing

约 7 分钟 · 与 世界谱 同源世界观 · 零网络

about 7 minutes · same world as the codex · fully offline

傍晚，阿汐看见指尖的灰淡了。她没有擦，也没有把它写进薄簿。盐仍在门槛边，干痕仍在船底。薄簿合着，页角偶尔翻动。她没有记下。有些淡去，不必补记。

水在灶上微微开了——它自己知道时候——她不去掀它——她不去动它——她只是听——她知道簿自己会翻——它也知道灰自己会淡——它们都在自己那儿漂着——她也在她自己那儿漂着。

· · ·

薄簿仍合着。——它合着是它的事——她不需要它开——册子里那一页自己会翻——它永远知道——她也不问它——她只是看——页角偶尔自己翻动一下——她笑一下——册子不需要她催——她也不需要催册子。

那粒盐——门槛上的——它还是老样子——从未挪动——也不需要挪动——她有时候看它——有时候不看——它都在那儿——它在那儿就够了——灰也一样——淡了的灰——它也在那儿就够了——册子也一样——页角偶尔自己翻动——它也就在那儿就够了。

· · ·

潮水又退去了——表盘不响——木板收紧了一根手指的宽度——她还是没有去看海——她只是坐在灶旁——亮了又暗——暗了又亮——册子又翻了一页——她笑一下——都懂了——都老朋友了。

母亲的声音回来——我的孩子——你瘦了——让它自己翻吧——它知道时候了——她说：好——她只是听——页角自己翻动了一下——她也跳了一下——都懂了——都老朋友了——补不补——她不补。

· · ·

她有时候看门——盐在那里——她知道它从未挪开过——灰也淡了一层——簿自己翻了一页——她忽然觉得——这三件都不需要——任何笔触——不需要记——不需要填——不需要补——她也不需要催——她只是她。

她有时候把手放在薄簿上——它凉凉的——页角又自己动了一下——它会——她也会——不缺不缺——它自己知道——她也知道——这就够了。

· · ·

她没有去看海。

有些页，不必替它翻。

止音纪元没有结束。它只是又多了一个傍晚——盐还在门槛，灰还淡着，册子自己翻到下一页——她也很好。

At dusk, Axi saw the ash on her fingertip fade. She neither wiped it away nor entered it in the thin ledger. The salt remained by the threshold; the dry mark remained beneath the boat. The ledger stayed shut, its page corner turning now and then. She recorded nothing. Some things fade without needing another entry.

The water was just beginning to boil — it knew its own hour — she did not lift the lid — she did not hurry it — she only listened — she knew the book would turn on its own — and that the ash would fade on its own — they drifted in their own ways — she drifted in her own way.

· · ·

The ledger stayed closed. — Its being closed was its own affair — she did not need it open — the page turned on its own — the book always knew — she did not ask it — she only watched — the page corner would turn now and then — she smiled once — the book did not need her to hurry — and she did not need to hurry the book.

That grain of salt — on the threshold — it remained as it always was — it had never moved — it did not need to — sometimes she glanced at it — sometimes she did not — it was always there — its being there was enough — so was the ash — faded as it was — its being there was enough — so was the book — the page corner turning on its own — its being shut was enough.

· · ·

The tide went out again — the dial never sounded — the boards tightened by one finger's width — she still did not look toward the sea — she only sat by the stove — bright then dim — dim then bright — the book turned one more page — she smiled once — everyone understood — old companions, all of them.

Her mother's voice came back — my child — you are thinner — let it turn on its own — it knows its own hour — she said: all right — she only listened — the page corner turned on its own — she leapt too — everyone understood — to keep or not — she would not record.

· · ·

Sometimes she watched the door — the salt was there — she knew it had never moved — the ash had faded by a shade — the page turned on its own — she suddenly felt — none of the three needed — any stroke — not filled — not remembered — not re-inked — neither did she — she was herself.

Sometimes she rested her hand on the thin ledger — it was cool — the page corner twitched once — it would — she would — not needing to — it knew — and so did she — that was enough.

· · ·

She did not look at the sea.

Some pages need not be turned.

The age of hush has not ended. It has only lived one more dusk — the salt still stood, the ash continued to fade, the book turned to the next page on its own — and she was well.
