未开之页

The Unopened Page

回廊钟第37年 · 门槛上的盐还在 · 她没有看它 · 也没有看海 · 页角平整 · 干痕仍在

The 37th year of the Arcade — the salt remained on the threshold; she rose neither for it nor for the sea; the dry mark outside waited

约 7 分钟 · 与 世界谱 同源世界观 · 零 LLM 撰写

about 7 minutes · same world as the codex · written without LLM assistance

门槛上的盐还在。阿汐没有看它，也没有看海。薄簿合着，页角平整。夜里潮水退去，门外多了一道浅浅的干痕。她没有起身。天亮时，干痕仍在，盐粒旁边空着一处。

她把手放在门槛上——木板的纹理在晨光里一条一条地亮着——那种没有谁去数过的纹路。她伸手想去摸一下那道干痕——但她没有伸手。不是她没想伸手——是她知道伸手就会让它变成手的一部分——而她想让它继续是它自己的东西。

· · ·

薄簿仍合着。薄页——她把它看作一页海的信封——什么时候会打开，她从来没有被告诉过。她渐渐懂了，她根本不用被告诉。她只是等——等没有人需要教的一件事。

影子某天又短了一寸。或者不是短了——是淡了一寸。她没有站起来问——她知道如果问，它就变成了她必须决定要不要回答的问题。她就继续等——看着它本身就够了。

· · ·

渡人某天说：有些页不写，也是纸在呼吸。他说他看见过一本册子，它整本都在替它本来应该装着的东西呼吸。页没有颤——页只是呼吸。

她想——纸在呼吸——她用了很久才明白。薄页没翻开——页面没被人读过——可薄页每一秒都在变。纸张也会厌倦——或者不——纸也许不会厌倦，只是时间会穿过纸，把纸磨得更薄……或者更厚。她没有去查那个是哪种。

· · ·

她没有去看海。

有些页，不必翻开才算留下。

止音纪元没有结束。它只是终于翻开了它自己的薄页——没人记得什么时候翻的。

The salt remained on the threshold. Axi looked neither at it nor at the sea. The ledger was closed, its page corner flat. At night the tide receded, leaving a faint dry mark outside the door. She did not rise. By dawn it remained, with an empty space beside the grain.

She kept her hand on the threshold — the boards' grain lit up in the morning light, one line at a time, uncounted. She wanted to reach and touch the dry mark — but she didn't. Not because she didn't want to — she knew that to touch it would make it part of her hand — and she wanted it to keep belonging to itself.

· · ·

The ledger stayed closed. The page — she thought of it as a letter the sea had never sent — when it would open, no one had ever told her. She had come slowly to know she did not need to be told. She only waited — waited for the one thing no one had ever had to teach her.

The shadow had shrunk by an inch, or rather, had faded by an inch. She did not rise to ask — she knew that if she asked, it would become a question she would have to decide to answer. She kept waiting — watching it was enough.

· · ·

One day the ferryman said: some pages, unwritten, are paper breathing. He said he had once seen a book that breathed for everything it should have held. The pages never trembled — the pages only breathed.

She thought about it — paper breathing — it took her a long time. The page did not open — no one read it — and yet the page was changing every second. Paper can grow tired too — or perhaps not — perhaps it simply allows time to wear through it, thinner and thinner… or thicker. She didn't investigate which.

· · ·

She did not look at the sea.

Some pages remain without being opened.

The age of hush has not ended. It has only, at last, opened its own thin page — and no one remembers when.
