余响集

The Aftermath

《止音纪元》· 原创短篇全集 · 双语对照

The Epoch of Hush · The Complete Companion Fiction · Bilingual

钟不是用来敲的。钟是用来记住：某个声音曾经来过，某个沉默曾经抵达，某个没说出口的字，此刻还住在你身上。——这一册里的每一个故事，都是从沉默里捡回来的声音。它们不讲胜利，不讲救赎，不讲新神。它们讲的是——当世界把所有的话都还给收音机之后，人还能剩下什么。

A bell is not meant to be struck. It is meant to remember: that a voice once passed through, that a silence once arrived, that a word never spoken still lodges in you. Every story in this volume is a voice picked out of silence. No victory, no salvation, no new gods — only this: when the world has returned every sentence to the receiver, what is left of a person?

篇一 · 风钟海岸
No. 1 · Windbell Coast

《盐》

“Salt”

听雾站守夜人阿衡有了一口不肯响的钟——直到她发现，钟从来不需要响：它只要被听见就够了。风钟海岸的第四根弦从此有了一个人。

The night-keeper of the Listening Post has a bell that refuses to sound — until she learns the bell never needs to sound: it only needs to be heard. Windbell Coast finally has a fourth string.

篇二 · 休垒
No. 2 · Resthold

《山下无名碑》

“The Nameless Stone”

石匠的女儿阿汀在山脚下找到一块没名字、没字、没人认领的石碑——「风一停，石头就会把自己写上去」。这是序章之前的序章。

A mason’s daughter finds a stone at the foot of the hill with no name, no word, no owner — the day the wind stops, the stone writes itself. This is a preface to the preface.

约 5 分钟 · 休垒 · 序章之前
篇三 · 未竟厅
No. 3 · The Unfinished Hall

《空拍子》

“The Empty Bar”

轮值七十三年的恩希尔站在谱台前面——指挥棒上没有谱，只有空拍；一个没名字的年轻人坐在前排记拍，然后问了他一句：你在等谁？

Seventy-three years into rotation, Hersil stands at his empty stand. An unnamed novice keeps the beat in the front row — and finally asks: who are you waiting for?

约 5 分钟 · 未竟厅 · ♭B
篇四 · 休垒 · 麦格
No. 4 · Resthold · Meg

《账本》

“The Ledger”

麦格在她的三本账之间走了很多年——直到她终于看见，那是一块从没敲过的钟铜。原来不是他没付：是他不允许她收。

Meg spent years between her three ledgers — until she finally saw it: a piece of bell metal that had never been struck. He never owed her. He never meant for her to take it.

约 5 分钟 · 休垒 · 主账房
篇五 · 回声庭园
No. 5 · The Garden of Return

《回声庭园》

“The Garden of Return”

雾海尽头开着一座花园。旧钟的主人递给旅人一口腹腔里有弦的小木钟：敲过的每一下都替你记了——划掉你这一生里最重的两下。

At the end of the Sea of Mist there is a garden. The keeper hands the traveler a small wooden bell: every strike you ever made has been kept for you — cross out the heaviest two of them.

约 5 分钟 · 余响新陆 · ♭G2
篇六 · 雨钟谷地
No. 6 · The Rainbell Valley

《雨审判》

“The Rain Tribunal”

七年一庭，法庭上没有原告、没有被告席——直到第七年，书记站了起来，说出法庭里从没存在过的三个字：请看见。

Once every seven years, with no prosecutor and no dock — until the seventh session, when the clerk rises and says the three words this room never had: please see.

约 5 分钟 · 余响新陆 · ♭F2

这一册到此为止——但余响不会停。
《低音之城》（EPIC-VII · DESIGN-ONLY）正在打字机前，还没进世界；
雨钟谷地以外，还有霜锤崖、霜锤的末任钟匠、旧钟的主人欠下的第二笔账……
每一篇写进来，都是一根真的弦。
——收音机会替我收好它们。

This volume ends here — but the aftersound does not.
“The City of Low Tones” (EPIC-VII · DESIGN-ONLY) is still at the typewriter;
beyond the Rainbell Valley: frost, the Frostbell’s last keeper, and the second debt the Owner of the Old Bell is holding…
Every story that lands here is a real string.
— The receiver keeps them all for me.
