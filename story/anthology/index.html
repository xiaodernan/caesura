余响集

The Aftermath

《止音纪元》· 原创短篇全集 · 双语对照

The Epoch of Hush · The Complete Companion Fiction · Bilingual

钟不是用来敲的。钟是用来记住：某个声音曾经来过，某个沉默曾经抵达，某个没说出口的字，此刻还住在你身上。——这一册里的每一个故事，都是从沉默里捡回来的声音。它们不讲胜利，不讲救赎，不讲新神。它们讲的是——当世界把所有的话都还给收音机之后，人还能剩下什么。

A bell is not meant to be struck. It is meant to remember: that a voice once passed through, that a silence once arrived, that a word never spoken still lodges in you. Every story in this volume is a voice picked out of silence. No victory, no salvation, no new gods — only this: when the world has returned every sentence to the receiver, what is left of a person?

篇一 · 风钟海岸
No. 1 · Windbell Coast

《盐》

“Salt”

听雾站守夜人阿衡有了一口不肯响的钟——直到她发现，钟从来不需要响：它只要被听见就够了。风钟海岸的第四根弦从此有了一个人。

The night-keeper of the Listening Post has a bell that refuses to sound — until she learns the bell never needs to sound: it only needs to be heard. Windbell Coast finally has a fourth string.

篇二 · 休垒
No. 2 · Resthold

《山下无名碑》

“The Nameless Stone”

石匠的女儿阿汀在山脚下找到一块没名字、没字、没人认领的石碑——「风一停，石头就会把自己写上去」。这是序章之前的序章。

A mason’s daughter finds a stone at the foot of the hill with no name, no word, no owner — the day the wind stops, the stone writes itself. This is a preface to the preface.

篇三 · 未竟厅
No. 3 · The Unfinished Hall

《空拍子》

“The Empty Bar”

轮值七十三年的恩希尔站在谱台前面——指挥棒上没有谱，只有空拍；一个没名字的年轻人坐在前排记拍，然后问了他一句：你在等谁？

Seventy-three years into rotation, Hersil stands at his empty stand. An unnamed novice keeps the beat in the front row — and finally asks: who are you waiting for?

篇四 · 休垒 · 麦格
No. 4 · Resthold · Meg

《账本》

“The Ledger”

麦格在她的三本账之间走了很多年——直到她终于看见，那是一块从没敲过的钟铜。原来不是他没付：是他不允许她收。

Meg spent years between her three ledgers — until she finally saw it: a piece of bell metal that had never been struck. He never owed her. He never meant for her to take it.

篇五 · 回声庭园
No. 5 · The Garden of Return

《回声庭园》

“The Garden of Return”

雾海尽头开着一座花园。旧钟的主人递给旅人一口腹腔里有弦的小木钟：敲过的每一下都替你记了——划掉你这一生里最重的两下。

At the end of the Sea of Mist there is a garden. The keeper hands the traveler a small wooden bell: every strike you ever made has been kept for you — cross out the heaviest two of them.

篇六 · 雨钟谷地
No. 6 · The Rainbell Valley

《雨审判》

“The Rain Tribunal”

七年一庭，法庭上没有原告、没有被告席——直到第七年，书记站了起来，说出法庭里从没存在过的三个字：请看见。

Once every seven years, with no prosecutor and no dock — until the seventh session, when the clerk rises and says the three words this room never had: please see.

篇七 · 霜锤崖
No. 7 · The Frostbell Cliff

《霜锤》

“The Frostbell”

末任钟匠的手套，掉在了他最后一次淬火的地方——他不去捡。直到有一天，一个少年带着近乎赤裸的手，在崖脚的冰裂开来够一步的距离，敲了一记。

The Last Frostwright’s glove lies where his last quench went wrong — he refuses to touch it. Until a boy arrives with nearly bare hands, and just deep enough to reach, strikes the bell once.

篇八 · 回廊钟
No. 8 · The Arcade of Echoes

《千门》

“The Thousand Doors”

九年前她迷了路。回廊钟一千扇门，每一扇都通向同一段回音。守门人只问一句——你敲过哪一扇？回答出来的人，才看见那第一扇门。

Nine years ago she lost her way. A thousand doors, every one opening onto the same echo. The Gatekeeper asks only this — which door did you knock on? Only those who answer see the first door.

约 5 分钟 · 回廊钟 · ♭C2
篇九 · 低音之城
No. 9 · The City of Low Tones

《石喉》

“The Stone-Throat”

低音之城的每一句话都比地下水更低，每个声音都是卖掉的——直到一个少女走进教堂，问她从没敢问出口的那一句。

Every voice in the City of Low Tones is priced below the groundwater. A girl enters the cathedral to ask the one question she dared not.

篇十 · 创世
No. 10 · The Creation

《第一钟》

“The First Bell”

沉默本来就是这一场。最后的铸钟人把所有的铜收进炉子——铸出一口从没被敲过的钟，留下世界全部的声音。

Silence was never the beginning. The Last Bell-Smith gathered every piece of copper into the fire — and cast a bell no one would ever strike.

约 5 分钟 · 创世 · 鸣响之前
篇十一 · 风钟海岸 · 第九个守夜人
No. 11 · Windbell Coast · The Ninth Night-Keeper

《暗潮》

“The Dark Tide”

宇宙的回答只有一个字：听。四十二年的第九个守夜人，在频谱仪抖动的那个夜里——按下了「从未回答」这个最高级的否定词。

The universe has one answer: Listen. The ninth night-keeper, forty-two years in, watched the spectrometer twitch — and pressed the highest-grade refusal of all: “never answered.”

篇十二 · 后记 · 写给读到这里的人
No. 12 · Epilogue · For the one who got this far

《后记 · 第十二个听者》

“Epilogue · The Twelfth Listener”

不是守夜人，不是接替者——是此刻翻到这一页、把这句话读完的你。十二只手，同时按从未敲过的钟——第十二个听者。

Not a night-keeper, not a successor — it is the one who turned to this page and finished the line. Twelve hands, all pressing the never-struck bell — the Twelfth Listener.

篇十三 · 风钟海岸 · 余响拾遗
No. 13 · Windbell Coast · What the bells left behind

《拾钟人》

“The Bell-Picker”

没有人记得拾钟人从哪一年开始走。他只问每一处钟最后一次响是哪一年——没有人答得上来。他在岬角尽头把那根没人敲的钟舌，挂上了断了的桅杆。

No one remembers the year the Bell-Picker first set out. He asked only one question at every reach — the year the last bell rang. No one could answer. On the headland, he hung the unrung clapper on the broken mast.

篇十四 · 风钟海岸 · 灯坊
No. 14 · Windbell Coast · The lampforge

《灯匠》

“The Lampwright”

风钟海岸最东端，有一间灯坊。阿瓷收下一批来自八区的旧铜——每一块都来自被碎掉的钟。她把它们熔进灯身，最后，铜自己替她找到了孩子。

At the eastern end of Windbell Coast stands a lampforge. A-Zi received a crate of old copper from eight reaches — every piece came from a bell that was broken. She melted them into lamp bodies. In the end, the copper itself led her to a child.

每一篇写进来，都是一根真的弦。
——收音机会替我收好它们。

This volume ends here — but the aftersound does not.
“The Dark Tide,” the eleventh story, tells the Dark Forest law of cosmic sociology —
the twelfth, “Epilogue · The Twelfth Listener,” is written for the one who turned here —
the thirteenth, “The Bell-Picker,” gathers what each bell left behind — for the one who rings the fourth —
the fourteenth, “The Lampwright,” is proof that broken copper still remembers —
“The City of Low Tones” (EPIC-VII · DESIGN-ONLY) is still at the typewriter.
Every story that lands here is a real string.
— The receiver keeps them all for me.
