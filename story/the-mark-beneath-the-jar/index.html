壶底旧痕

The Mark Beneath the Jar

回廊钟第37年 · 圈印最深处有一道极细的纹路 · 盐粒始终站着 · 她没有把壶移远

The 37th year of the Arcade — a fine line within the ring; the grain of salt still standing; she never moved the jar away

约 7 分钟 · 与 世界谱 同源世界观 · 零网络

about 7 minutes · same world as the codex · fully offline

清晨，阿汐移开水壶，木板上留着一圈浅印。盐粒没有滚动，印子也没有合拢。薄簿仍合着，船仍停在门外。船底的干痕没有变。她没有数那圈印，也没有把壶移远。

这一回她留意到圈印的最深处——那里有一道极细的纹路，像一根没有拉过的弦——她蹲下去了一会儿——没有碰它——她心想：有些痕迹也不急着被谁认出来。她直起腰时，天已经亮了一格。她拍了拍膝盖上的灰——仿佛替那道纹路说了一声：别急。

· · ·

那粒盐仍立在圈印边缘——它没有滚动——仿佛它天生就站在那儿。她把它想象成一名站岗的小兵——它也不需要交接——它就是那样立着——一天一天——她习惯了。

薄簿仍合着。——她有一次把手按在封面上——停住了——她想：它里面肯定记录了所有没被说出口的事——也许一个字都没有——也许全是——她没有再问自己——她把手收了回去。

· · ·

潮水来了又退。——她没有出门。——她听见木板极轻微地——收紧了半分。——她知道它在收紧。——她也不知道它为什么收紧。——她只是继续添柴。

母亲的话没有回来。——这回是她自己先开口——对着窗外那只不动的船——她说：行。——然后她才意识到自己说了出来——厨房里静了一瞬——然后灶火又跳了一下。

· · ·

她没有去看海。

有些线，只在两处之间留下。

止音纪元没有结束。它只是终于留下一道不急着被认出来的细痕——和一粒始终站着的盐。

At dawn, Axi moved the water jar, leaving a pale ring on the boards. The grain of salt did not roll, and the ring did not close. The ledger stayed shut; the boat remained outside, its dry mark unchanged. She did not count the ring or move the jar away.

This time she noticed the deepest point of the ring — a very fine line ran there, like a string never drawn — she crouched for a while — did not touch it — thinking: some traces are not eager to be named by anyone either. When she stood back up, the sky had brightened by one notch. She brushed the dust from her knees — as if to tell that line: take your time.

· · ·

That grain of salt still stood at the ring's edge — it had not rolled — as though it had been born standing. She imagined it as a small sentry at its post — it needed no relief — it simply stood — day after day — and she grew used to it.

The ledger stayed closed. — Once, she laid her hand upon its cover — and stopped — she thought: it must hold every unsaid thing — perhaps not a single word inside — perhaps all of them — she did not ask herself further — she drew her hand back.

· · ·

The tide came and went again. — She did not go out. — She heard the boards, very faintly, — tighten by a half-notch. — She knew they had tightened. — She did not know why. — She only kept feeding the fire.

Her mother's phrase did not return. — This time she spoke first — toward the motionless boat — she said: all right. — Only then did she realise she had said it aloud — the kitchen went quiet for a moment — then the flame leapt once more.

· · ·

She did not look at the sea.

Some lines remain only between two places.

The age of hush has not ended. It has only, at last, left behind a fine mark never eager to be named — and a grain of salt forever standing watch.
