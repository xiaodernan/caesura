门槛之外

Beyond the Threshold

回廊钟第37年 · 影子仍停在门外 · 她没有请它进来 · 门没有关

The 37th year of the Arcade — a shadow remains at the threshold; she neither weighs it nor invites it in; the door stays open

约 7 分钟 · 与 世界谱 同源世界观 · 零网络

about 7 minutes · same world as the codex · fully offline

影子仍停在门外。阿汐没有称它，也没有请它进来。潮水退去，门槛前留下一道湿痕；天亮后，湿痕淡了，位置没有变。她偶尔想关。她那只握住门扇的手——有几次，她甚至已经感到门在移动——可影子没有动。它就站在原地，像海的某段沉默直接撞上来。她也就没有推动门。

· · ·

薄簿的页角仍然自己偶尔翻一回。她以前以为是风吹的——她如今以为是海在称她的记性有多重。薄页没有回答她。它只是偶尔翻过去，偶尔不翻。薄页也许正在决定那一页对不对得上影子。

影子的轮廓在有时候很清楚，很透亮——像海的另一面有人举着一盏湿灯。湿灯不照东西。它只是让它亮一下子。每次之后，影子就大一寸，或者小一寸。她没有去量。

薄簿偶尔自己翻过去——她没有去干预它。她慢慢明白过来：薄簿的页角和海的潮汐一直在合伙数同一个数，比世界的计数方法来得慢，也比老人眼睛看外面的时候慢。她以前总想让它们快一点，好让她早一步知道下一步会发生什么。

如今她学会了不催。她只是等——等它自己走完自己的路，无论它是谁，无论它要走多久。

· · ·

她慢慢知道：它来过；也去过。它没有需要被记下的事。它是另一场称量——比薄簿的任何一页都轻，又都重。薄簿合着，仍放在接缝旁。门没有关。

· · ·

她没有关门。

有些来处，不必立刻登记。

止音纪元没有结束。它只是终于学会了说「等」——等的意思是：不追也不挡。

The shadow remained outside. Axi neither weighed it nor invited it in. When the tide withdrew, a wet mark stayed before the threshold. By dawn it had faded, but its place remained. Now and then she thought of closing it. The hand that held the door — a few times she had felt the door begin to move — but the shadow did not move. It kept where it was, the way some silence of the sea collides head-on. She did not push the door.

· · ·

Now and then the page corner of the ledger still turned on its own. She once thought the wind was doing it; she now suspected the sea was weighing her own memory. The page did not answer. It only sometimes turned, sometimes didn't — as if it were deciding whether that page matched the shadow.

The shadow's outline was sometimes startlingly clear — as though, from the sea's far side, someone were holding a wet lantern. The lantern does not light things. It only lets them shine for a breath. After each time, the shadow grew a thumb larger — or a thumb smaller. She did not measure.

Now and then the open page of the ledger turned by itself. She used to think the wind was doing it — she stopped interfering with it. She understood that the page and the sea kept an old agreement; they had nothing to say to each other, and each knew the other was there.

Now she had learned how not to hurry them. She only waited — let whatever-it-was walk its own road out, whoever it was, however long it would take.

· · ·

She came slowly to know: it had come; and it had gone. It had nothing that needed entering. It was another kind of weighing — lighter and heavier than any page of the ledger. The ledger lay closed, still beside the seam. The door stayed open.

· · ·

She did not close the door.

Some arrivals need not be entered at once.

The age of hush has not ended. It has only, at last, learned a character — 等， to wait — which means to neither chase nor block.
