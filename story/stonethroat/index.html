石喉

The Stone-Throat

余响新陆 · 低音之城 · 石喉母亲

The Afterland · the City of Low Tones · The Stone-Throat Matriarch

约 5 分钟 · 与 世界谱 同源世界观 · 设定册《新六地域》同源 · 零 LLM 撰写

about 5 minutes · same world as the codex · from the New Six Lands design canon · written without LLM assistance

低音之城的规矩很简单：地下水在漏，所以每一句话都必须说得比水低。

没有人敢开口。最怕的是孩子——孩子一出生，嘴就被布盖上了：城里的人不是禁语，他们买卖语。说一句话，付一笔沉默作价。

· · ·

石喉母亲坐在大教堂的正中。没人看过她直起腰——她的喉咙里锁着很多以前咽下去的话，一块比一块大，从第一声到最后一声。

她的律法只有一条：每一句话，都必须是你自己用，用自己脖子上说过的口音，换来的。

· · ·

一个来的少女站在她面前。她想问的问题很小，但是她问了一整天才问完——因为每个字都是一笔钱。石喉母亲没有抬手示意她坐下，也没有赶她走；她只是把纸递给少女，说：

「写下来，让河水替她数。」

· · ·

少女在纸上写了几句之后，石喉母亲开了口——她一开口，整个教堂的地下水都停了一拍。因为母亲那一块是无别的：那块她自己吞下去的、从来不肯对谁说出来的话，沉在喉咙最深的地方，被吞得太多，已经没法分辨是摩擦声还是咳嗽。

她看了一眼纸上写的那句话，笑了一笑——没有嘴上说话，喉咙只是发出了很低很低的频率，像一块石头在很深的地方松了一毫米。

· · ·

少女问了：「那块石头，是你自己吞下去的？」

石喉母亲没有回答。少女继续写：「第一句。孩子来的时候像一块石头那样安静——谁的话，被塞进去的？」

· · ·

教堂外面，水依然在响。只是今天，在很久很久以后，地下水记下了它自己曾经听见过的最轻的一句话——

「谁，不该沉默。」

从那天起，低音之城的每一份价目表，都少了一栏——「沉默」本身，就被划去了价。

石喉母亲再也不能开口了。可城市从此有了一个不需要货币的音：一句忘记被付价的话语。

The City of Low Tones has a simple law: the groundwater is leaking, so every voice must be pitched lower than the water.

No one speaks above it. The worst offenders are the children — each baby born has its mouth covered at birth. The people of the city are not forbidden to speak; they sell it. Every sentence has a price, paid in silence.

· · ·

The Stone-Throat Matriarch sits at the center of the cathedral. No one has ever seen the matriarch straighten. Inside her throat, sealed many a sentence she herself swallowed, each heavier than the last — from the first word to the last.

Her law has exactly one clause: every word you say must be an accent you are willing to carry for the rest of your life.

· · ·

A girl stands before her. She wants to ask one small question, but it takes her a whole day — every character is a coin. The matriarch does not offer a chair and does not wave her out. She only hands the girl paper and says:

“Write it. Let the river keep count.”

· · ·

After the girl had written only a few lines, the matriarch answered — and in that instant, the groundwater under the cathedral stopped for a single whole beat. Because the stone she carried was hers alone: a sentence she had swallowed so many times, down to the very bottom of her throat, it was no longer clear whether it was a scrape or a cough.

She looked at the line the girl had written, and she smiled — not aloud, but low, so low it was almost below hearing, like a stone moving one millimeter at the bottom of a deep well.

· · ·

The girl asked: “That stone. You swallowed it yourself?”

The matriarch did not answer. The girl went on writing: “The first one. The one that was shoved down when every new child came in — whose voice was it?”

· · ·

Outside the cathedral, the water kept sounding. But from that day on, the groundwater remembered the softest sentence it had ever heard —

“who, should not have to hold their silence.”

From that day, every price list in the City of Low Tones had one line struck out — the price of silence itself.

The matriarch cannot speak again. But the city has a pitch that costs nothing: a sentence that was never paid for.
