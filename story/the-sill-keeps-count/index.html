窗台记数

The Sill Keeps Count

回廊钟第37年 · 盐线两半并在窗台 · 中间空处刚好放一枚铜扣 · 退潮也是一种记数

The 37th year of the Arcade — the two halves of the salt line, the empty space between them, and the ebb tide that keeps count

约 7 分钟 · 与 世界谱 同源世界观 · 零 LLM 撰写

about 7 minutes · same world as the codex · written without LLM assistance

纸袋还在门边。窗台上的盐线没有再长。她把两半并在一起，中间留着一道空处——刚好能放下一枚铜扣，也刚好能放下一个字。清晨，空处仍在。

阿汐不再动它。连擦窗台的时候，她都只擦它的左半和右半，中间那道空处留给它自己——它在等，还是不在等，谁都不知道。只有一件事确定：窗台每天早上替她看着它。

· · ·

那天起，她自己也开始记数。阿沉的旧刻度还在——一拃、半拃、消失。他的「记」字被风吹歪过一个角。阿汐没有再新画刻度；她只在每天的粉笔末端，轻轻点一下窗台板。

第一天，三个点；第二天，五个点；第三天，她忍不住笑了。她发现自己居然在数——可数什么，为什么，她自己也不知道。也许是为了让那段时间有东西留下；也许是为了不被等到失去耐心。

有一天傍晚她走到海滩边，看见自己走过的地方，沙上也添了新的半圆——海水退潮时也会留下一串很浅的点。她蹲下来看了很久，站起来的时候，她才明白：退潮也是一种记数。它没有刻度，却从不重复。

· · ·

第三个清晨，空处里多了一道极淡的影子——像远处海面上碎开的光、像很老的粉笔的最后一笔的影子。阿汐看了一眼，没有喊阿沉，也没有数。她只在空处旁边，添了一个很短很短的划。

那根划和阿沉那三条线并排立着——一根点三下，一根画过去，一根被风吹歪。沉默把它们收进同一幅画。

· · ·

风又来的时候，纸袋没有动。窗台上两半并在一起，空处还在那儿。那晚她走了一趟海滩——回来的时候，鞋底沾着一点盐。

门没有开。空处也没有填上。

止音纪元没有结束。它在学两个字——等。

The paper bag stayed by the door. The salt line on the sill did not grow again. She placed its two halves together, leaving an empty space between them — just wide enough for a copper button, just wide enough for a single character. In the morning, the space remained.

Axi did not touch it anymore. Even when she wiped the sill, she wiped left, then right, leaving the empty space to its own business — whether it waited, or did not wait, no one could say; only that the sill watched it for her, every morning.

· · ·

From that day, she kept count too. A-Chen's old ticks were still there — one hand's width, half, gone. His 记 had been tilted by the wind. Axi drew no new ticks; she simply touched the pencil against the sill each morning, a small point of graphite for each day she had waited.

The first day, three points. The second, five. On the third she caught herself and smiled. She had started to count — what she was counting, and why, even she did not know. Perhaps to leave a mark of the waiting; perhaps to spare the waiting from losing its shape.

One evening she walked to the beach. Where she had stepped, the sand bore a few new half-circles — the sea left its own faint points when the tide went out. She crouched and stayed a long time, and when she stood up she understood: the ebb is also a kind of counting, without ticks, never repeating.

· · ·

On the third morning a very faint shadow lay across the empty space — like light broken into silver on a distant sea, like the ghost of a very old chalk stroke. Axi saw it, said nothing to A-Chen, and did not count. She only drew one more short stroke beside the empty space.

The new stroke stood beside A-Chen's three — one tick-mark, one drawn line, one bent by wind. Silence folded them all into the same picture.

· · ·

When the wind came again, the bag did not move. On the sill, the two halves lay together and the space remained. That night she walked to the beach; when she came back, there was salt on the sole of her shoe.

The door stayed shut. The space was not filled.

The age of hush has not ended. It is learning two characters — wait.
