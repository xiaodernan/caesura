空秤

The Empty Scale

回廊钟第37年 · 秤盘空着也盛着 · 有些记下来的事不必写进簿 · 盐粒没有秤过任何东西

The 37th year of the Arcade — the pan empty and holding; a single grain of salt the ledger will not enter

约 7 分钟 · 与 世界谱 同源世界观 · 零 LLM 撰写

about 7 minutes · same world as the codex · written without LLM assistance

次日，天亮时它就在那里——秤盘里的盐。渡人没有带走秤，阿汐也没有把盐放回海里。潮水来过，秤盘湿了，盐粒仍留在原处。木板的其他地方都干了，只有那一小块干处下面的地方还是凉的。

她早上一醒就跑来看它。铜秤还放在门槛上；铜扣、铁钉和薄簿都一动不动；秤盘里的盐粒仍在。她把它看了很久，像看一朵换了很多次姿势的小花。

· · ·

渡人某天回来的时候，阿汐问他：这个盐要怎么称？他站在门口看了一会儿，然后说：它已经不用称了——它自己会称自己的。阿汐没听懂那句话，可是她把它记住了。

渡人没有上门槛去。他在门口站了一会儿，就走了。那一次他没有停。

· · ·

海又来过几次，潮水也涨了几回。薄簿一直躺在接缝旁，薄页一直没有被记。她偶尔会把薄簿拿起来看看，然后又放下。她发现薄页和海之间有一种很老很老的协议：谁都打开它的时候，谁都可以合上它。

她把薄簿合上，没有记这一页。

· · ·

薄簿的薄页偶尔自己翻过去一页。她以前以为是风吹的——后来她不再去干预它了。她觉得薄页和海合伙开的那一页，谁都不是等着谁。它们之间没有话，可是都知道对方在的。

有一天晚上她在门口站到天黑。木板开始发凉的时候，她才起身回去。她没有关门——留一条缝就够了，留了很多很多年，没有问题。

· · ·

她以前觉得「空」是一种缺什么。现在她懂了：空是一种装着。

秤盘空着，也盛着。

止音纪元没有结束。它只是终于留下了一架永远不称物的秤——和一粒永远不秤自己的盐。

The next day, it was there before the first light — the grain of salt on the pan. The ferryman had not taken the scale, and Axi had not returned the salt to the sea. The tide came; the pan grew wet, but the grain stayed. The boards everywhere else had dried by morning, and the place beneath the dry patch stayed cool.

She ran to it first thing. The brass scale was still on the threshold; the button, the nail, and the ledger lay as they always lay; the grain was in the pan. She studied it for a long time, the way one watches a small flower that has changed its posture.

· · ·

One day the ferryman returned. Axi asked him: how is that salt to be weighed? He stood in the doorway for a moment and said: it no longer needs to be weighed — it weighs itself. She did not understand the sentence, but she remembered it.

The ferryman did not step across the threshold. He stayed by the door for a while, and then he left. He did not come in that day.

· · ·

The tide came again; again the ledger lay by the seam; the open page was never entered. Now and then she lifted the ledger and looked at it — and then set it down again. She had come to understand that the page and the sea had an old agreement: either party could open it, and either could close it.

She closed the ledger without entering this page.

· · ·

Now and then the open page of the ledger turned by itself. She used to think the wind was doing it — she stopped interfering with it. She understood that the page and the sea kept an old agreement; neither was waiting for the other; they had nothing to say to each other, and each knew the other was there.

One evening she stood in the doorway until it was black. When the boards began to cool, she finally went back inside. She did not close the door — a single seam would be enough, and it had been enough for many years.

· · ·

She used to think "empty" meant "lacking". She understood now: empty is a kind of holding.

The pan was empty, and held something.

The age of hush has not ended. It has only left behind, at last, a scale that will never weigh anything — and one grain of salt that will never weigh itself.
