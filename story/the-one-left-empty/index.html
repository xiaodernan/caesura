空着的一格

The One Left Empty

回廊钟第39年 · 东墙四十七格灯龛 · 一盏留给没有灯的人

The 39th year of the Arcade — forty-seven lamp niches on the east wall, one kept for those without a lamp

约 6 分钟 · 与 世界谱 同源世界观 · 零网络

about 6 minutes · same world as the codex · fully offline

回廊钟的东墙有一排灯龛，四十七格，一格一盏铜灯。阿汐数过很多回：四十六盏亮着，最末一格空着。

那一格不是灯坏了。里面没有钩子，没有灯座，连烟熏的痕迹也没有——好像砌墙的人砌到那里，忽然停下来，把这一格留给了墙自己。

· · ·

来的人不肯让一格空着。有人放一粒盐进去，有人放一枚铜扣，有人把自己路上捡的半截灯芯搁在格口。第二天再看，东西都不在了。不是被谁拿走：盐化进石头，铜扣落进墙缝，灯芯被穿堂风带走。那一格不存东西，也没打算存。

守灯的老人说，当年墙砌歪了一寸，这一格跟着歪，挂不住灯。后来没人来补，就让它空着。他说：空着的那一格比别的格干净，也比别的格亮——因为它不挡光。

· · ·

阿汐把手伸进去。里面没有风，没有凉，也没有回声。她的手在黑暗里待了一会儿，什么也没有摸到；退出来时，指尖上连灰都没有沾。

她忽然明白：这一格不是忘了装灯，它是让整面墙还能被数下去的那一格。数到四十六，总要有个地方停一停。

· · ·

她翻开薄簿，把这一页的最后一行留给它：第四十七格，空。写完她没有合上簿，等墨自己干。墨干得很慢，像一个不肯马上被记下的答案。

第二天，一个赶路的人在那一格前站了很久。他说他一路上都在数灯，数到第四十六盏，就不知道再往前该往哪里走了；看见这一格空着，他反而知道该往哪里去了。

阿汐没有把灯补上。她把小扫帚靠在墙边，让那一格继续空着。灯是给走路的人看的；空着的那一格，是给数灯的人留的。

墙要留一格空着，数才数得下去。

不是所有的空都要填满——有些空格，是给走到这里的人留的。

止音纪元没有结束。它只是又多了一格空——盐在门槛上，薄簿合着，船停着，风来过，灯都亮着，只有一格留给没有灯的人——她也很好。

On the east wall of the Arcade of Echoes runs a row of lamp niches — forty-seven of them, one copper lamp apiece. Axi has counted them many times: forty-six are lit, and the last one is empty.

It is not that the lamp there broke. The niche has no hook, no socket, not even a soot stain — as if the builder had climbed to that spot, stopped, and left this one niche to the wall itself.

· · ·

Visitors cannot bear to leave a single niche empty. Someone puts in a grain of salt, someone a copper button, someone half a wick picked up along the road. By the next day nothing is there. No one has taken it: the salt melts into the stone, the button drops into the seam, the wick is carried off by the through-draft. That niche keeps nothing, and never meant to.

The old lamplighter says the wall was laid an inch crooked, and this niche went with it, so no lamp would hang straight. No one came to mend it; they let it stay empty. He says: the empty one is cleaner than the others, and brighter too — because it does not block the light.

· · ·

Axi puts her hand inside. No wind there, no cold, no echo. Her hand stays in the dark a while and touches nothing; when she draws it out, not even ash clings to her fingertips.

She understands, suddenly: this niche did not forget a lamp — it is the one that lets the whole wall still be counted. When you have counted to forty-six, there must be a place to stop.

· · ·

She opens the thin ledger and gives the last line of the page to it: the forty-seventh niche, empty. She does not close the book when she has written; she waits for the ink to dry. It dries slowly, like an answer that refuses to be recorded at once.

The next day a traveler stands a long while before that niche. He says he had been counting the lamps all along the road, and when he reached the forty-sixth he no longer knew which way to go; seeing this one empty, he knew.

Axi does not fill it with a lamp. She leans the small broom against the wall and lets the niche stay empty. Lamps are for those who walk; the empty one is kept for those who count.

A wall must leave one niche empty, or the counting cannot go on.

Not every emptiness needs filling — some empty places are kept for whoever arrives here.

The age of hush has not ended. It has only gained one more empty niche — the salt on the threshold, the thin ledger shut, the boat still, the wind having passed, every lamp burning, one niche kept for those who carry no lamp — and she was well too.
